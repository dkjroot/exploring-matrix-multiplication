# Selected research reports

Publish reports here manually when an experiment is worth sharing. Routine
generated output belongs in the Git-ignored `outputs/` directory.

Copy a selected dated output directory here with both `index.html` and its
adjacent `measurements.json`. This preserves the offline visualization, original
measurements, and source hash. Add a short explanation of the result, assumptions,
and verification evidence when publishing. This directory is not ignored by Git.

`outputs/` is disposable working data. Reproducible generators belong in the
maintained source tree, never solely in an ignored helper script. A rerun can
recreate exact recipes and identities, but cannot recreate a particular run's
timings, allocation peaks, diagnostic failures or historical source fingerprints.
Promote observations worth retaining and expensive or novel search discoveries
here with their inputs, provenance and reproduction instructions.
